# Visa site acceptance matrix

## Home route `#/`

### Hero

- Desktop: quote, four achievement cards, then a full-width CTA below them.
- Mobile: compact headings and no clipped achievement text.
- CTA scrolls to `#services`.

### About

- Mobile: statistic icons are hidden.
- Statistic values stay on one line, including `2 000+`.
- Labels and supporting text have enough width and do not collide with values.

### Safe Case method

- Mobile: pillar icons are vertically centered against their text cards.
- Desktop: image and pillar column have balanced width.
- Complex-case images are hidden below the tablet breakpoint and visible on desktop.
- Complex-case labels are left-aligned and vertically centered inside equal-height blocks.

### Services

- Mobile opens on the popular middle tariff.
- Part of a neighboring card is visible as a swipe cue.
- Finger or pointer drag changes the selected tariff.
- Arrow buttons and pagination dots select all three tariffs.
- Desktop shows three cards; the popular tariff is centered.

### Success stories and reviews

- Personal data must be checked before publishing final replacement images.
- Opening and closing an image does not permanently stop automatic movement.

### Blog preview

- `Ира Соболева про визы` fits on one mobile line at 390 px.
- The home preview remains compact and links to `#/blog`.

## Blog route `#/blog`

- Header title uses at most two lines on mobile.
- Desktop cards include an image, type/date metadata, title, and description.
- At 1440 px, four cards appear in one row.
- Telegram placeholders say that links will be added later and are not clickable fake links.
