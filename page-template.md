# Page Structure Template: Conversion-Oriented Landing

Follow this sequence of blocks to maintain the "Safe Case" methodology logic and high conversion rates.

## Block 1: Hero Section
- **Goal**: Immediate value proposition and trust.
- **Elements**: 
  - Expert photo (high quality).
  - Big headline (Name + Promise).
  - Sub-headline (Specific result).
  - Primary CTA (Assessment/Contact).
  - Floating trust badges (Stats/Method name).

## Block 2: About / Authority
- **Goal**: Establish the expert's background.
- **Elements**: 
  - Personal story/path.
  - Statistics grid (Numbers that impress).
  - Iconic representation of experience.

## Block 3: Visual Proof (Slider)
- **Goal**: Social proof through tangible results.
- **Elements**: 
  - Horizontal slider of visa photos.
  - Auto-scrolling + Navigation controls.

## Block 4: Methodology (The "Safe Case")
- **Goal**: Explain the unique selling point (USP).
- **Elements**: 
  - Logical pillars (3-step process).
  - Complex cases list (Whom this is for).
  - Visual hierarchy emphasizing "safety" and "logic".

## Block 5: Social Media / Community
- **Goal**: Show active presence and mass trust.
- **Elements**: 
  - Follower count.
  - Links to platforms (IG, TG, YT).
  - Content highlights.

## Block 6: Services & Pricing
- **Goal**: Conversion and selection.
- **Elements**: 
  - Pricing cards (Compare features).
  - Toggles for add-ons (Support/VIP).
  - Clear "What's included" list.

## Block 7: Reviews
- **Goal**: Emotional proof.
- **Elements**: 
  - Screenshots of chats/feedback.
  - Immersive dark container for focus.

## Block 8: Guarantees & Risk Reversal
- **Goal**: Remove final objections.
- **Elements**: 
  - Refund/Redo policy.
  - Security/Legal assurance.

## Block 9: Final CTA (Contact)
- **Goal**: Direct lead generation.
- **Elements**: 
  - Friendly invitation to chat.
  - Direct Messenger link (Telegram).

## Block 10: Footer
- **Goal**: Navigation and legal info.
- **Elements**: 
  - Contact info.
  - Legal links (Privacy, Offer).
